# account_move

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP. The naming convention (e.g., `account_move`, `journal_id`, `partner_id`, `invoice_date_due`) and the specific column patterns (e.g., `inalterable_hash`, `move_type`, `create_uid`) are characteristic of the Odoo accounting module's core ledger structure.

## Functional process 
This table supports the "Record-to-Report" and "Order-to-Cash" business processes. It serves as the central ledger for all accounting entries, including customer invoices, vendor bills, bank statements, and manual journal entries. It tracks the lifecycle of financial documents from creation through to payment reconciliation and tax reporting.

## Description
One row in this table represents a single accounting entry or "move" within the general ledger. It captures the header-level details of a transaction, including its financial state, associated partner, currency, and total monetary values. In the staging layer, this represents a raw, near-real-time copy of the Odoo `account.move` model, serving as the foundation for downstream financial reporting and sub-ledger reconciliation.

## Caveats for downstream consumers

- **Sensitive Data:** Contains `invoice_source_email` and `invoice_partner_display_name`, which may be considered PII.
- **Timestamps:** `create_date` and `write_date` are stored in UTC.
- **Soft Deletes:** Odoo typically does not use soft deletes for `account_move`; records are usually marked as `state = 'cancel'` rather than removed.
- **Signed Amounts:** Fields ending in `_signed` are critical for financial reporting as they correctly handle debits/credits based on the move type. Always prefer these over raw `amount_*` fields for ledger calculations.
