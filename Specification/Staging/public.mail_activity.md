# mail_activity

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo (formerly OpenERP), an open-source ERP system. The naming convention (e.g., `res_model_id`, `res_id`, `create_uid`, `write_uid`) and the specific structure of tracking activities against generic business objects are characteristic of the Odoo framework's ORM layer.

## Functional process 
This table supports the "Activity Management" or "CRM/Task Tracking" process. It tracks scheduled tasks, calls, meetings, or emails associated with various business entities (like leads, opportunities, or invoices) across the platform, allowing users to manage follow-ups and monitor completion statuses.

## Description
One row in this table represents a single scheduled or completed activity instance linked to a specific business record. It serves as a raw landed copy of the Odoo `mail.activity` model, capturing the grain of one activity per user per business object.

## Caveats for downstream consumers

- **Sensitive Data:** The `note` column may contain PII or internal communication details; ensure appropriate masking if exposed to non-authorized users.
- **Timezones:** Timestamps (`create_date`, `write_date`) are typically stored in UTC in Odoo, but `user_tz` is provided for context.
- **Soft Deletes:** The `active` column acts as a soft-delete flag. Queries should generally filter by `WHERE active = TRUE` unless performing historical audits.
- **Data Grain:** The `res_model` and `res_id` columns form a polymorphic relationship; ensure joins are handled by filtering on both the model name and the ID.
