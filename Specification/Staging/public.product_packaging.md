## Functional process 
This table supports the inventory and logistics management process, specifically defining how products are packaged for procurement and sales. It links specific products to their packaging configurations, including barcodes and quantities, to facilitate warehouse operations and supply chain tracking.

## Description
One row in this table represents a single packaging configuration for a specific product, defining attributes such as the quantity contained and the associated barcode. As a staging table, it serves as a raw, landed copy of the Odoo `product.packaging` model, capturing the state of packaging definitions as they exist in the source ERP.
