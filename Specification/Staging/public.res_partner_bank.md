# res_partner_bank

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo (formerly OpenERP), an open-source ERP system. The naming convention `res_partner_bank` is standard for Odoo's "Resources" module, which manages partner (customer/vendor) master data and their associated financial account details.

## Functional process 
This table supports the financial master data management process, specifically the storage of bank account information linked to business partners. It facilitates payment processing, vendor disbursements, and customer receipt reconciliation by maintaining a registry of valid bank accounts, routing numbers, and account holder details.

## Description
One row in this table represents a single bank account record associated with a specific business partner. It serves as a raw landing copy of the Odoo `res.partner.bank` model, capturing the relationship between partners and their financial institutions. The grain is one row per bank account record.

## Keys

- **Primary key (inferred):** `id`
- **Foreign keys (inferred):** 
    - `partner_id` → `res_partner.id` (Standard Odoo relationship to the partner entity).
    - `bank_id` → `res_bank.id` (Standard Odoo relationship to the bank registry).
    - `currency_id` → `res_currency.id` (Standard Odoo relationship to currency definitions).
    - `company_id` → `res_company.id` (Standard Odoo relationship for multi-company scoping).
- **Natural keys (inferred):** 
    - `acc_number` (Combined with `partner_id` and `company_id` usually forms the business-level uniqueness).

## Caveats for downstream consumers

- **Sensitive Data:** The `acc_number` and `acc_holder_name` columns contain PII and financial data; ensure these are masked or restricted in downstream reporting layers.
- **Timezones:** Timestamps (`create_date`, `write_date`) are stored in UTC by Odoo.
- **Soft Deletes:** The `active` column acts as a soft-delete flag; queries should generally filter by `WHERE active = TRUE` unless performing audit analysis.
- **Data Quality:** `sanitized_acc_number` should be preferred over `acc_number` for join operations or deduplication logic.
