# sale_order

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, as evidenced by the characteristic naming conventions such as `partner_id`, `company_id`, `journal_id`, and the use of `create_uid`/`write_uid` for audit tracking. The structure is typical of Odoo's `sale.order` model, which manages the end-to-end sales process.

## Functional process 
This table supports the Order-to-Cash (O2C) business process. It captures the lifecycle of a sales order from quotation to confirmation, including financial terms, shipping logistics, and tax calculations. It integrates with CRM (via `opportunity_id`), Inventory (via `warehouse_id`), and Accounting (via `journal_id` and `invoice_status`).

## Description
One row in this table represents a single sales order header, capturing the commercial agreement between the company and a partner. It acts as a raw landing copy of the Odoo `sale.order` model, maintaining the grain of one order per unique identifier. It serves as the primary source for downstream sales reporting, revenue recognition, and order fulfillment tracking.

## Caveats for downstream consumers

- **Timestamps:** All timestamps are assumed to be in UTC as per standard Odoo/PostgreSQL configurations.
- **Soft Deletes:** Odoo typically does not use soft deletes; records are usually permanent unless explicitly removed.
- **Sensitive Data:** `access_token` and `signed_by` should be handled with care regarding data privacy.
- **Precision:** `NUMERIC` types are used for financial fields; ensure downstream systems handle these with appropriate decimal precision to avoid rounding errors.
- **JSONB:** The `customizable_pdf_form_fields` column contains semi-structured data; ensure your ETL process can parse JSONB if extracting specific attributes.
