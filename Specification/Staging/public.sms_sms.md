# sms_sms

## Source system
This table originates from an Odoo ERP system, as evidenced by the naming convention (`sms_sms`), the use of `mail_message_id` (linking to Odoo's `mail.message` model), and the standard Odoo audit columns (`create_uid`, `write_uid`, `create_date`, `write_date`).

## Functional process 
This table supports the SMS communication module within the ERP, tracking the lifecycle of outgoing text messages. It manages the queueing, sending, and status tracking of messages sent to partners or external phone numbers, often integrated with the broader notification system.

## Description
One row represents a single SMS message record, capturing its content, destination, and current delivery status. As a staging table, it serves as a raw, direct copy of the Odoo `sms.sms` model, preserving the state of messages for downstream reporting or audit trails.

## Caveats for downstream consumers

- **Sensitive Data:** The `number` and `body` columns may contain PII. Ensure appropriate masking or access control is applied.
- **Timezone:** Timestamps are typically stored in UTC in Odoo; verify against the specific instance configuration.
- **Soft Deletes:** The `to_delete` column suggests that records may be logically deleted rather than physically removed; filter by `to_delete = FALSE` for active records.
- **Data Integrity:** As a staging table, this data is raw; expect potential inconsistencies in `number` formatting (e.g., missing country codes).
