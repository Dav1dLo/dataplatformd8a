# stock_move_line

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo (ERP), as evidenced by the characteristic naming conventions such as `picking_id`, `move_id`, `product_uom_id`, and the presence of `create_uid`/`write_uid` audit columns which are standard in the Odoo ORM framework.

## Functional process 
This table supports the inventory management and logistics process, specifically tracking the granular movement of stock items between locations. It records the actual execution of inventory transfers, linking products to specific lots, packages, and source/destination locations within the warehouse.

## Description
One row in this table represents a single line item of a stock movement, detailing the quantity of a specific product moved between two locations. It serves as a raw landed copy of the Odoo `stock.move.line` model, capturing the audit trail of inventory transactions at the most granular level.
